# Manuscript revision

After drafting, complete four passes in order.

## Facts

Map every number, quote, event, and product capability to source IDs. Distinguish official claims, independent evidence, real trials, and judgment. Remove unsupported precision; record a blocker when missing evidence changes the conclusion.

## Structure

State each paragraph's new information in one sentence and merge repetition. Headings answer the reader's current question. The opening reaches the subject promptly; the ending provides boundaries, action, or a next observation.

## Language

Replace abstract reporting with who did what, cost, and outcome. Split sentences until subjects/actions are clear. Remove repeated slogans, formulaic transitions, unsupported scenes, and atmosphere-only metaphors. Read aloud to repair awkward order, ambiguous references, and excessive punctuation.

## Delivery

Check frontmatter, author, topic label, H1, and fixed components. Run check_prose.py; clear errors and review each warning. Check placeholders, broken images, undefined abbreviations, and unexplained time/measurement scope. Record actual checks/results; a draft is not publication.
