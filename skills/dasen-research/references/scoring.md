# Candidate ranking

Scores rank candidates; they do not replace editorial judgment. Adjust weights for the brief; the default base is 100 points.

| Dimension | Points | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| Reader problem | 25 | Specific scenario and real recurring need |
| Deliverable | 25 | Action, result, template, judgment, or verifiable case |
| Evidence | 20 | Adequate originals, independent sources, trials, timestamps |
| Timeliness / durability | 15 | Urgent response or sustained reuse |
| Added information | 15 | New facts, methods, outcomes, or boundaries |

Recommend scores at least 80 for briefing. Keep 60–79 only with explicit gaps, usually evidence or narrower scope. Below 60 stays out of the current queue. Return fewer candidates rather than lowering the threshold to fill a quota.

Modifiers: actual local/real-scenario results +8; reproducible artifact +8; explicit failure/cost/applicability boundary +5; untested pure list -20; reference article replacing factual evidence -20; heavily repeated topic without new material -10. Unverified volatile product/price facts block selection; an unlocatable URL/local source is discarded.

Merge cross-platform reports of one event into one candidate with multiple sources. At most two candidates per account avoids treating one author's routine as a field-wide trend. Similar titles with distinct deliverables may remain; identical deliverables merge.
